ABOUT

Hi! I'm an industrial designer. My practice specialises in user - oriented design, achieving ergonomic function and emotional resonance through deliberate form and color choices. I am passionate about sustainability - driven design approaches, including modular systems, repairability, and structural efficiency. I hold a BFA with honors in Industrial Design from the University of Illinois at Urbana - Champaign and an MA in Design Products from the Royal College of Art.
